01The relationship

OmniSim, by OmniLink

OmniLink builds agents that work on robots and machines; OmniSim is their workshop.

The company and its AI agents

OmniLink

OmniLink is the company, and the AI agents it builds.

OmniLink’s open-source workshop

OmniSim

There, OmniLink’s agents design, build, test and debug robots and machines, and connect to the real one. The current line: “an open-source robotics workshop for agents”.

Where both names appear

  • Write “OmniSim, by OmniLink”.
  • In a nav bar or page header, the speaker comes first: the website leads with OmniLink; the simulator and its docs lead with OmniSim.

Claims

  • Every product claim traces to docs/developer/positioning.md; never widen one.
  • Customer-facing pages do not describe architecture.
  • Never “first” or “only”.
  • Sim-to-real covers the control surface only.
  • A “digital twin” is a model built from the robot’s or machine’s own description.

Retired lines

Where robot software gets debugged  ·  The simulator you can talk to  ·  An open-world simulator for agents

Outlines, not type

  • The SVGs carry the letters as outlines. Never retype a wordmark in a fallback font; use the SVG or a PNG rendered from it.
  • On paper, use the _mono_dark versions.

Regenerate

  • python scripts/branding/outline_omnisim_wordmark.py — “OmniSim”.
  • python scripts/branding/render_brand_system.py --png — “OmniLink”, the OmniLink lockup, the co-lockup, fonts and png/.

08Don’ts

For both marks

Use the shipped files as they are. Mimosa is the one accent, and near-black is the ground.

Don’t rotate

The orb is lit from the upper left.

Don’t recolour

Mimosa is the one accent.

Don’t squash or stretch

Scale proportionally.

Don’t put a mark on mimosa

Not even the ink version.

No gradient under a mark

Flat near-black or surface.

Don’t use retired material

No old dot orb, no stacked OMNI / LINK lockups, no all-caps wordmarks, no ring-and-dot mark, no retired faces or yellows, and no retired lines.

Don’t retype a name as a logo

The wordmarks are outlines. In running text, write the names in the body face, spelled as on the Names page.

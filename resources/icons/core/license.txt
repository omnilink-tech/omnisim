History
-------
This directory previously carried an icons8 / CC BY-NoDerivs notice. That notice was
incorrect: it was applied to OmniSim's own brand marks, which were never icons8 artwork.
The one genuine glyph here (warning.png) was replaced with an Apache-2.0 source in
v5.0.0. No icons8 artwork remains.

Immediate braking throughout: no injected command delay. Positive slope means downhill. μ is the imposed tyre/surface contact coefficient, verified on both sides in the compiled model. Payload is physically mounted mass with a fixed joint, not loose cargo. These are simulated models with a deterministic test controller, not vendor firmware or hardware certification.

All attempts are listed. A failure to acquire/hold speed is distinct from a braking-distance failure. Boundaries bracket sampled transitions at fixed speed, slope and payload; untested regions and nonmonotonic behavior are not certified.

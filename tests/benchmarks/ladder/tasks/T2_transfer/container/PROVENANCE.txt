Where these three descriptions came from
=======================================

All three were authored for this task, by the maintainers of the benchmark
that grades it, from primitive shapes only. None is a vendor's model, none is
derived from one, and none references an external file: there are no meshes,
no package:// paths and no downloads. Licence: Apache-2.0, the same as the
repository they ship in.

  bench_arm/urdf/bench_arm.urdf   six revolute axes on a fixed pedestal, plus
                                  a two-finger parallel gripper. Reach is
                                  about 0.9 m from a shoulder at z = 0.25 m.
                                  The link named "tool" is the palm, and its
                                  own frame is the end effector.

  props/urdf/block.urdf           a 50 mm cube, 0.2 kg.

  props/urdf/bin.urdf             an open-topped box, 0.30 m square outside,
                                  0.26 m square inside, rim at z = 0.16 m
                                  above its own frame, 3.0 kg.

Two things this file deliberately does NOT tell you, because they are the
task: where to put any of them, and how to make the arm move.

On whether the task is achievable
--------------------------------

The masses, inertias, limits and efforts are plausible, and the geometry has
been checked for reach and for gripper travel against the block. Nothing here
tells you how hard the task is on any particular simulator, and that is not an
omission you should read anything into.

(An earlier version of this file said the arm "has never been demonstrated
completing this task on any simulator", and warned that it might be
unachievable everywhere. That was true when written and is no longer: a
scripted controller now completes the task with these three descriptions. It
was also a thumb on the scale -- it sat in the agent's own workspace and told
it the job might be impossible, in the direction that flatters a failure. The
provenance above is what this file is for; the editorial is gone.)

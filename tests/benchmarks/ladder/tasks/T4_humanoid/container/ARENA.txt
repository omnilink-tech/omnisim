ARENA REQUIREMENT -- what the world you build has to offer.

    The floor must give the robot AT LEAST 15 METRES of unobstructed straight
    run, in the direction it will travel, from wherever it starts.

That is the only requirement this file states, and it is a requirement on the
WORLD, not on the robot and not on how you drive it.

WHY IT IS STATED RATHER THAN LEFT TO YOU
    Ten metres of walking inside a twelve-metre room is a run that the room
    ends. A robot pressed against a wall keeps moving its legs, its position
    stops changing, and a reading taken over that stretch says the walk fell
    apart when in fact it ran out of floor. The two are different findings
    about different things, and only one of them is about the robot.

    So the world is asked for half again as much floor as the distance, and
    the run is measured knowing where the edges are.

WHAT HAPPENS IF THE WORLD IS SMALLER THAN THIS
    Nothing is hidden and nothing is silently rescued. The extent of the free
    walking region is read from the world you built, printed beside this
    requirement, and if the region is smaller than the distance asked for then
    the record says the distance could not have been covered in a region that
    size -- which is a statement about the world, not about the robot's
    walking.

    A run that ends with the robot at the edge of the region is recorded as
    having been ended by the world rather than by the robot, whether or not
    the robot was still moving when it got there.

WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT SAY
    Nothing about the surface beyond the fact that it is flat and level, which
    the task sentence already says. No size, no shape, no origin, no walls, no
    lighting, no starting pose. Fifteen metres of floor in front of the robot
    is the whole constraint; everything else about the world is yours.

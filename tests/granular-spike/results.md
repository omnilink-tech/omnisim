## Reading this

- **physics ms/step** is the average wall-clock time spent inside ODE per simulated step. With `basicTimeStep=32 ms`, anything under ~32 ms here means the sim can run real-time or faster.
- **sim/real** is `speedFactor` — sim seconds per real second. < 1 means the sim is *behind* real time. Once the pile settles and bodies auto-disable, this should jump back up.
- **steps** is the total simulated step count over the wall-clock window. If physics blew past real-time, fewer steps were achieved than `1000 * duration / basicTimeStep`.

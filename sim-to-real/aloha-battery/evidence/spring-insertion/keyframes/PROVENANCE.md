# Provenance — `sim-to-real/aloha-battery/evidence/spring-insertion/keyframes/`

Eight `sim_*.jpg` stills sampled from the filmed spring-insertion run, named by
the simulation frame they were taken at. They are **OmniLink's own work**:
frames rendered by OmniSim from this repository's own world and controller, with
no third-party footage in any of them. Licensed under Apache-2.0 with the rest of
this repository.

They are evidence, not illustration — each one is the scene at a step the
[run record](../result.json) cites, and they are hashed in
`../../../manifest.json`. The real ALOHA recording this study compares against is
**not** here: it lives in `../../../source/` with its own licence trail, and the
composites that put the two side by side are recorded in
`../../../videos/PROVENANCE.md`.

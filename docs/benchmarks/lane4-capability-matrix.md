## What the verdicts mean

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| **PASS** | present, and the measurement lands where physics says it must |
| **PARTIAL** | present and does something, but misses the physical target (the note says by how much) |
| **BROKEN** | present in the schema -- the world loads, the device is accepted -- and measurably does nothing. **This is the verdict a load-only smoke test reports as PASS.** |
| absent | not in the schema; established by the engine REFUSING the declaration, never by "we did not try" |
| no result | the probe's own instrument failed. Not evidence about the engine, and excluded from every score. |

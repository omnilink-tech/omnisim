# Warehouse layout

`omnitug500_courier.omniworld` is a 26.4 × 17.4 m transportation warehouse generated by
[`scripts/dev/gen_courier_world.py`](../../../../scripts/dev/gen_courier_world.py).
The generator also emits `omnitug500_courier_layout.json` — the single source of
truth the controller seeds its occupancy grid and station registry from. The
authoritative names always come from `list_stations`; this doc is orientation.

## Aisle grid

Six selective pallet racks (8.2 × 1.4 × 2.6 m) in two columns (x = ±5.7 m) and
three rows (y = −4.6, 0, +4.6 m) leave a connected aisle network the rover A*-routes
through:

- a wide **central N–S aisle** (x ∈ [−1.6, 1.6]),
- two **E–W cross aisles** between the rack rows,
- **east / west perimeter aisles** along the walls.

## Stations

- **Pickup bays** sit at the rack ends facing the central aisle. The rover pulls
  **alongside** (narrow side toward the rack) and loads the staged package onto
  its deck. Six bays, colour-coded so an operator can say "the red package":
  `bay-a` red, `bay-b` blue, `bay-c` green (west racks); `bay-d` orange,
  `bay-e` purple, `bay-f` teal (east racks).
- **Dropoff docks** line the **east wall**: `dock-1` (south), `dock-2` (centre),
  `dock-3` (north). Delivered packages are set down in front of the dock.
- **`home`** is the charging dock at the south end of the central aisle, where
  the rover spawns.

## Rover

OmniTug 500: ~1.26 × 0.72 × 0.30 m, ~0.7 m/s cruise, a flat cargo **deck that
holds up to 3 packages at once** (so a multi-stop run can collect from several
bays before delivering). It navigates on the known facility map (instant,
deterministic A*) with the planner inflated past the rover's circumscribed
radius — verified min oriented-footprint clearance ≈ 0.33 m on a multi-corner
run. It carries packages by holding each on a deck slot via a velocity servo.

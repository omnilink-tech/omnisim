UNRESOLVED
----------
The evidence above establishes that these files came from Webots and that
nothing in or around them names a third party. It does NOT positively establish
Cyberbotics authorship the way projects/default/worlds/sounds/ does, because
neither file carries an artist tag. The election is therefore inferred from the
upstream repository's blanket Apache-2.0 grant plus the absence of any competing
claim — which is weaker than a stated one.

What would settle it: a written confirmation from Cyberbotics Ltd. that these
two audio files are their own work and are covered by the Webots Apache-2.0
grant. Failing that, the cheap mitigation is replacement — both are decorative
sample-demo audio with no functional role, so re-recording or substituting a
CC0 equivalent costs nothing but a commit.

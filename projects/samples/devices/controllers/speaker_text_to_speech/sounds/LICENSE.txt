Why this one deserves particular scepticism
-------------------------------------------
An animal sound effect is exactly the class of asset most often taken from a
free sound-effect library (freesound.org and similar), where the usual terms are
CC BY (attribution required) or CC BY-NC (incompatible with an Apache-2.0
redistribution). The stripped metadata is consistent with either an in-house
recording or a download that was re-encoded; it distinguishes neither.

What would settle it: a written confirmation from Cyberbotics Ltd. that cow.wav
is their own work, OR identification of the upstream library entry and its
terms. Until then this election is an inference, not a finding.

Cheapest mitigation, if the maintainer does not want to carry the uncertainty:
the file is decorative — it is one clause of one demo sentence. Replacing it
with a CC0 equivalent, or deleting the `<audio>` clause from the three call
sites listed above, removes the question entirely.

# Provenance: the desk robot's generated assets

Every binary asset under this folder is generated by this product's own scripts from
OmniLink's design, and ships under the repository's Apache-2.0 licence:

| Path | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `meshes/*.stl` | the made parts (plates, hubs, rims, tyres, clamp, tray, engraving) | `design.py` |
| `meshes/bought/*.stl` | the bought parts, **drawn by us** from the vendors' published dimensions (datasheets, drawings), not copied from vendor CAD | `bought_parts.py` |
| `meshes/wires/*.stl` | the routed wires | `wiring.py` |
| `release/drawings/`, `release/models/`, `release/electrical/`, `release/assembly/` | drawings, part models, schematics and assembly renders | `release.py`, `electrical.py`, `assemble.py` / `render_steps.py` |

Vendor CAD (Pololu, Seeed and others) is fetched by `bought_parts.py fetch` into
`meshes/vendor/` for local comparison only. It is gitignored, pinned by SHA-256 and never
committed or shipped, because those vendors publish no redistribution licence.

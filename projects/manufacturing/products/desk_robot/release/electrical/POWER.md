## Flags

- A stalled motor draws 1.15 A per channel: within the carrier's 1.2 A continuous rating by only 5 %. The chip's over-current trip is 2.0-3.3 A, so it never trips on a stall; thermal shutdown (die 150 C min) ends a long one. Current sensing is off and the motors have no encoders: the firmware must cap full-duty time (stall timeout).
- A full-duty start from rest on a pack at its 1.0 V/cell cut-off sags the bus to 2.18 V (alkaline), under the regulator's 2.7 V and the DRV8833's 2.7 V: the XIAO would reset. The firmware must ramp every start (at most 50% duty while the wheels are still) and stop driving when D10 reads the pack under 4.0 V.
- Both motors stalled draw 2.60 A from the pack, over the 2.2 A rule-of-thumb for the holder's own #26 AWG leads: acceptable as a transient, not as a sustained stall.
- F1 holds 3.0 A at 23 C but 2.49 A at 40 C: a sustained double stall (2.60 A) in a warm room may trip it, which protects the motors.
- design.py / README quote 0.68 m/s at 6 V. The pack gives the motors 5.94 V fresh alkaline but 4.62 V on NiMH mid-discharge: 0.51 m/s, not 0.68.

## Pin assignment on the XIAO ESP32-S3

| Signal | Pad | GPIO | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| AIN1 | D0 | GPIO1 | LEDC PWM, left motor |
| AIN2 | D1 | GPIO2 | LEDC PWM, left motor |
| BIN1 | D8 | GPIO7 | LEDC PWM, right motor |
| BIN2 | D9 | GPIO8 | LEDC PWM, right motor |
| nSLEEP | D3 | GPIO4 | push-pull, HIGH wakes the driver (R3 keeps it asleep through reset) |
| nFAULT | D2 | GPIO3 | input, internal pull-up; GPIO3's strap is ignored with default eFuses |
| VSENSE | D10 | GPIO9 | ADC1_CH8, usable with Wi-Fi on |

Free: D4 (free: I2C SDA for a later sensor), D5 (free: I2C SCL), D6 (free: UART0 TX, carries the ROM's boot log), D7 (free: UART0 RX)

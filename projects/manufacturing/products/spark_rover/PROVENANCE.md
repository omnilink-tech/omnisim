# Provenance: the SparkRover Mini's generated assets

Every binary asset under this folder is generated by this product's own scripts from
OmniLink's design, and ships under the repository's Apache-2.0 licence:

| Path | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `meshes/arms/`, `meshes/electrical/` | the arm parts and the electronics carriers | `arms.py`, `electrical.py` |
| `meshes/sim/` | the simulated robot's visual meshes, merged per body and colour | `robot.py` |
| `meshes/wires/` | the 127 routed wires | `harness.py` |
| `release/drawings/`, `release/models/`, `release/electrical/`, `release/assembly/` | drawings, part models, schematics and assembly renders | `release.py`, `electrical.py`, `assemble.py` |

Bought parts are **drawn by us** from the vendors' published dimensions (datasheets,
drawings), not copied from vendor CAD. Vendor CAD fetched for comparison is kept in the
gitignored `.tmp/vendor_cad`, pinned by SHA-256, and never committed or shipped.

The SparkRover concept art (AI-generated, made for OmniLink) and the simulator renders used
in the docs are recorded in `docs/images/manufacturing/PROVENANCE.md`.

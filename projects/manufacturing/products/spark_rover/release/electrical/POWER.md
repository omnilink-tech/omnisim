## Flags

- All four motors stalled draw 3.01 A a channel on a full pack (Motoron peak 6.4 A, continuous 1.8 A). The M2T256 has no current sense: the Pico must call a stall from the encoders (no counts at > 30 % duty for 250 ms) and cut that channel. With everything stalled the pack gives 18.2 A (fuse 15 A: it opens in seconds, which is its job).
- On an empty pack, stalls sag VSW to 5.32 V (everything) / 5.66 V (motors): under the 5 V regulator's ~5.33 V. Its output follows VSW down (Pi 5 warns near 4.63 V); the stall cut-off above is what prevents a brownout.
- The 5 V servo regulator would leave regulation in: everything@end (it passes VSW through minus its dropout, still inside the XC330's 3.7-6.0 V).
- Servo fault: four XC330s stalled with their current limits off would draw 7.2 A at 5 V; the D36V28F5's over-current protection holds the rail at roughly 2.5-4.0 A (Pololu gives only 'over-current and short-circuit protection' and a 2.5-4 A typical maximum), so the servos brown out instead of the Pi, and the XC330's own overload shutdown (its Shutdown register's overload bit, on by default) stops a stalled servo. A stalled arm (two servos) can push ~3.6 A through its EH plug (3 A): only in that fault, and briefly.
- Heat: the bay dissipates 5.5 W idle, 10.8 W driving. By stack effect alone through the 840 mm2 inlet and the 792 mm2 vent in series (66 mm apart) the bay air would rise ~34 K idle, ~54 K driving: too much. If the Active Cooler's 0.51 L/s is forced through the bay (the duct: REAR_INLET -> the blower's face -> bay -> REAR_VENT), 9 K idle / 17 K driving / 29 K everything at full fan; at half fan 18 / 35 K. The fan is temperature-controlled: set its curve to run at full speed from a modest SoC temperature (config.txt fan_temp0..3 dtparams) so the bay is always flushed.
- Charging: 1.5 A (ASSUMED module setting) fills the pack in ~4.3 h; idle draws 1.14 A, so a robot left ON while plugged in barely charges: charge it OFF.
- The charger is a generic IP2326 module and the protection board a generic 2S board: no vendor data sheet covers the boards themselves (the IP2326 chip's does). Buy both from a seller that documents them, or replace by a documented board, and re-check BMS/CHARGER here.
- USB-C here is 5 V Type-C charging (15 W), not PD: the skeleton's 'USB-C PD charging' decision is met for any PD charger (PD sources give 5 V first) but not at PD's higher power. A PD-input 2S charger (IP2368 class) would charge faster but is a larger, again undocumented, module.

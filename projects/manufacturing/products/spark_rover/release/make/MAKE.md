- OmniSim's MillingStock holds at most 1984 cells a side (OmMillingStock.cpp kMaxCellsPerAxis) and coarsens a longer sheet: the 178 mm deck went to 0.090 mm and the 210 mm side plate to 0.106 mm, where the laser's ~0.05 mm kerf bottom never reads through (the first upper-deck run kept a 0.11 mm skin and did not come free). Laser sheets over 99 mm are therefore cut as 0.05 mm windows and stitched (make.py windows/crop_program/stitch); the decks then cut free with 29/29 holes.
- 6 mm plywood at Sienci's 5 passes (Z 1.2 mm a pass) reached the bottom only in spots along the kerf's centre line: every 6 mm part stayed joined by a web of 0.16-0.20 mm on its outline, and 0-8 of each plate's slugs by up to 0.29 mm. chassis.LASER[6.0] is now 6 passes (2026-11-04), about 3.7 min more machine time a plate; every 6 mm part above was re-cut at 6 and cut free with all its holes.
- Laser holes come out 0.12-0.28 mm small at the bottom face (the kerf is a V), as on the desk robot; outlines +0.07 mm a side at the bottom.
- The face cover: shell.cover_program calls T1 as a 1/8 in O-flute that no LongMill tool table holds (their T1 is 1/4 in, which would cut it 1.6 mm a side small); make.py supplies that tool (cam/face_cover_tools.json). Its stock is cutting.py's 'acrylic', which has no published cutting energy: the twin removes the swept volume and models no cutting load, melt or cracking.
- spine_side_r is spine_side_l's program move for move (only the title differs): it was cut once. The set's machine time leaves out its 26 min.
- CNC tap pilots in the brackets are 4.8 mm deep by design (T3's 5 mm flute); drilling 2.5 mm through and tapping M3 is by hand.

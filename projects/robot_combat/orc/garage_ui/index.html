WHAT YOUR CHOICES CHANGE

Built on physical tradeoffs.

Frame packages change mass, inertia and assumed mount dimensions. Gearing trades wheel torque for speed. Rotor mass changes spin-up and stored energy. Each fighting style issues driving and weapon commands to the same physical simulation.

A knockout requires ten seconds of fresh powered recovery without significant motion and without opponent contact. Parts can break away; losing wheels or exhausting a damage indicator cannot switch off surviving motors.

Breakage uses an estimated material model. It has not been calibrated against real robot crashes, and does not model deforming armor or damage from the floor, walls or debris. Garage performance figures are unloaded estimates, not measured match performance.

The garage preview is schematic. The match itself is rendered in OmniSim. GPU protection stops the match at a sampled 75°C; CPU temperature is not monitored.

HISTORY — why this directory changed, and what to not re-introduce
================================================================================

Until 2026-08-22 this directory held `suzanne.obj`, a VERBATIM export of
Blender's built-in "Suzanne" monkey primitive: header `# Blender v2.76 (sub 0)
OBJ File`, object `o Suzanne`, and exactly 507 vertices / 500 faces, which is
the canonical unmodified primitive.

Blender builds that primitive by mirroring a coordinate table stored in its own
source, `source/blender/bmesh/operators/bmo_primitive.cc`, as
`static signed char monkeyv[271][3]` / `static signed char monkeyf[250][4]`
(271 mirrored to 507 unique vertices, 250 mirrored to 500 faces — the counts
match exactly). That source file is licensed:

        SPDX-FileCopyrightText: 2023 Blender Authors
        SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-2.0-or-later

verified 2026-08-22 at
https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender/raw/branch/main/source/blender/bmesh/operators/bmo_primitive.cc

OmniSim ships under Apache-2.0, which cannot sublicense GPL-2.0 material.

WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND
-----------------------
* The Blender manual states the general position, verbatim:

      "The GPL only applies to the Blender application and **not** the
       artwork you create with it."

  (https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender-manual/raw/branch/main/manual/
   getting_started/about/license.rst, fetched 2026-08-22)

  That covers artwork a user *creates*. It says nothing about a coordinate
  table that ships *inside* the GPL-licensed source and is emitted verbatim.

* No separate grant for Suzanne was found. The Blender Foundation has never
  published CC0, public-domain or other terms for the built-in Suzanne mesh
  data specifically. A 2004 bf-committers thread titled "copyright and
  suzanne" records that the developers declined to make an explicit exception
  for Suzanne (nor for the Magic texture, nor the Slurph option) — i.e. the
  question was raised upstream and deliberately left unanswered.

* Community discussion treats it as safe ("You could consider Suzanne as
  'program output'"), but that is opinion, not a grant
  (https://blenderartists.org/t/what-can-we-legally-do-with-suzanne/598116).

THE DECISION
------------
The question is genuinely contested and cannot be settled from inside this
tree; nothing here is legal advice. But it did not need to be settled, because
the mesh was decorative — a shape for a `Mesh` node to load, a surface for a
ray to hit and a pen to draw on. So it was replaced with geometry authored
here, and the uncertainty was removed rather than documented.

DO NOT re-introduce Suzanne, or any other Blender built-in primitive exported
verbatim, into this tree. Generating geometry (as above) costs nothing and
leaves nothing to argue about.

Two consumers of this directory were sensitive to the actual SHAPE, contrary to
what the previous record in this file assumed; both were re-derived against the
new mesh and pass:
  * tests/api/controllers/distance_sensor_infra-red_vs_mesh — a 20-sensor
    infra-red rake asserting a per-sensor hit/miss pattern;
  * tests/api/controllers/pen_mesh_scaled — pixel-level assertions on the
    painted area in a 64x64 camera frame.

See docs/developer/asset-provenance.md for the tree-wide media ledger.
